# Creative-Caretakers

The Creative Caretakers team (MITES Saturdays 2026) developed an auditory aid that attaches to the Tandem Mobi insulin pump. We attempted to meet the need of elderly diabetics with macular degeneration. Many critical products such as glucose monitors and insulin pumps on the market do not account for vision loss. We invented a small portable device that would fit over the Tandem Mobi insulin pump to translate its visual signals to an auditory message. Due to the wide variety of closed source products on the market, we chose the Mobi because one of our group members had one. 

Our product is uses an ESP32-S3 (needed for I2S bus), RGB light sensors (to detect light signals), a speaker and amplifier (for audio output), a vibe motor (for haptic feedback), and a lipo battery. The design has a belt clip for easy portability.
